Five Crossings

Bush, sea, mountain, calf, giants — five thresholds Moses had to cross.

Series Overview

Moses didn't arrive at the promised land in a straight line — he crossed five thresholds, and each one tested a different part of him. The bush asked if he'd answer. The sea asked if he'd trust. The mountain asked if he'd wait. The calf asked if he'd hold the line. The giants asked if he'd believe. His crossings mirror our own.

Series Key Verse:

"Now all these things happened to them as examples, and they were written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the ages have come." — 1 Corinthians 10:11

The question that runs under every sermon: "What if it were you?" How would you have responded to God's call, faced the impossible, waited in the silence, remained faithful when God seemed absent, and confronted the giants standing between you and the promise?
